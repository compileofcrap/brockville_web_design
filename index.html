FAQ

Questions? Let's make this easy.

A few things people usually want to know before getting started.

Do I need to already own a domain?

No. I can help you choose a domain and connect it to your website.

Do I need to write all of my website content?

No. We'll figure out what information your customers need and organize the content together.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Mobile and desktop design are included in the package.

Will my website appear on Google?

I'll configure the website for Google Search and submit it for indexing. Search rankings and timing cannot be guaranteed.

Do you build online stores?

Ecommerce is a different type of project, so online stores are quoted separately.

Can you update my website after it launches?

Yes. Ongoing website updates and maintenance can be arranged separately based on our contract.
